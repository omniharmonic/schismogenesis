Privacy

The short version: your answers stay in your browser unless you choose to send them, nothing about you is public unless you switch it on, and you can delete everything.

What stays on your device

Your answers and result are computed in your browser and kept in its local storage so you can come back to them. Images you save are drawn in your browser.

If you make an account

An account lets you come back on any device and change your answers. You choose how to get in, and can add more ways later:

  • Bluesky or X: they tell us who you are, plus your name, handle and picture. We keep those and nothing else. We don't keep access tokens, and we never post or read anything for you.
  • Email: we keep your address to send you sign-in links and codes, through our email provider, Resend. We send nothing else, and never show the address to anyone.
  • A private key: we keep only a scrambled form of the key, so we can't recover it if you lose it.

We keep your answers for each debate, and a cookie that keeps you signed in on that device. That's the only cookie we set. Your answers also count, without your name, in the results.

The people map. Everyone who adds their answers can appear as a plain dot, with no name, placed by which thinkers they agree with most. Dots only appear once ten people have taken part. Your picture, name, short bio and handle appear only if you switch on “Show me on the map” in your account. Switch it off and they're gone within about ten minutes.

Deleting your account (in your account) removes your logins, answers, sessions and your answers from the results, straight away.

What we receive without an account, and only when you choose

  • Adding your answers to the results, or joining the live room, sends your answers (and any later votes) under a random ID. No name or email. A handle is optional and shown to no one. The results page shows only totals, and nothing about any group smaller than ten people.
  • A share link publishes the parts of your result you pick, after showing you exactly what. Anonymous links use a generated name. The print on a result-only link is drawn from your answers in a scrambled order, so it can't be read back into them.
  • A verified account: for Bluesky we read your public profile and recent posts to find the code you posted; for X, X tells us your name, handle and picture. We keep those three things and nothing else, and no access tokens.
  • Fairness ratings and misreading reports hold only what you write.

What we count

Daily totals only, such as how many people started or shared. No tracking cookies, no IDs, no third-party analytics. To stop abuse, we briefly keep a scrambled form of your network address; it can't be turned back into the address and is deleted after about a day.

Taking it back

Your share link can be deleted from the browser that made it, and deletion removes it from our database. To leave the room, use “Leave the room on this device”. For anything else, report it from your result page.

Age

This is for people 18 and over.
