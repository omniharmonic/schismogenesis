# Contemporary memetics and sensemaking groups: what they claim, what is shown, what Dialogos can take (as of 2026-11-01)

Evidence labels used throughout:
- **[SHOWN]** peer-reviewed or published study with data.
- **[PREPRINT]** working paper or preprint with data.
- **[SELF-REPORT]** the group's own number or description, not independently checked.
- **[ASSERTED]** a claim with no evidence offered in the source read.
- **[SECONDARY]** tertiary summary (Wikipedia, SEP) or search snippet, not the primary text.

Everything under "Inferences" is this researcher's reasoning, not a sourced fact. People are described only as they present themselves publicly; they/them is used throughout. ## Open Memetics Institute (OMI): who, what, method, evidence

### Takeaway
OMI is an informal network, not an institute in the normal sense. It has real, usable ideas (self-surveillance of networks, "no neutral culture scientist", dark vs open memetics) and one real data asset nearby (the Community Archive), but no published empirical results. Its "Anatomy of Internet Arguments" game is anecdotal. Treat it as a source of good questions and of possible collaborators, not as a method to import.

### Cited Findings
- [SELF-REPORT] OMI's stated goal is to "describe what are the minimum social protocols & technology necessary to do open memetic monitoring in your network". A network that does self-surveillance is an "open memetics network"; the site's analogy is that open memetics is to a collective mind what meditation is to one mind. — [OMI home](https://defenderofbasic.github.io/open-memetics-institute)
- [SELF-REPORT] Site source is under the GitHub account DefenderOfBasic; the home page itself names no operator. — [OMI home](https://defenderofbasic.github.io/open-memetics-institute)
- [SELF-REPORT] Core members listed: Defender's memetics lab (Katt, Faraday, Joshua, plus audiences), Emergent, Ben G, Nosilverv, Aleks Jakulin, Lincoln Sayger. Periphery includes Adam Aleksic, Brandon Duderstadt, Michael Smith ("morphenius"), Epistemic Garden (runs the Community Archive and the open memetics Discord), David Goldstein (open A/B testing of voters), Mike and Rhys of AskRally, Klew Studio, Indiana University OSoMe, Andy Ayrey. Funders listed: RonenV, Kanro, Analogue, Cosmos Grants. — [OMI members](https://defenderofbasic.github.io/open-memetics-institute/members.html)
- [SELF-REPORT] Funding details at snippet level only: a search snippet from the Defender notebook reads "$3.5k from Analogue Group, $50k from Kanro3, $20k from Tekne Labs / Ronenv" for 2025 ([notebook](https://defenderofbasic.github.io/notebook)), but the fetched notebook page did not show that line. The DefenderOfBasic "PhD" repo names Tekne Labs, Kanro and Analogue without amounts. — [PhD repo](https://github.com/DefenderOfBasic/PhD)
- [ASSERTED / eyewitness] Guy (@nosilverv), writing 2025-11-18 from Inkhaven at Lighthaven, reports three goals: make epistemic warfare visible, grow a bubble safe from it, and set up clean information highways among hierarchies. The "Janitor & CEO test" checks whether information fed to a janitor travels up a hierarchy accurately. Defender presented with Joshua at the Love Symposium on 2025-11-16. The post presents no empirical data, calls the talk "less than perfectly structured", says the author trusts their "taste", and leaves open whether this is a real field or crackpottery. — [Guy, "Open Memetics Institute"](https://rivalvoices.substack.com/p/open-memetics-institute)
- [ASSERTED] Same post: announced outputs are a slide deck (long-term plan, idea provenance problem, memetic safety debate, an "Open Memetics Journal", rigorous subjectivity, objects of study), whiteboard diagrams, and a first Unconference on Memetics in 2026. Also an "Egregoric Diversity Quotient" self-test. — [same](https://rivalvoices.substack.com/p/open-memetics-institute)
- [SELF-REPORT] Glossary: **dark memetics** is research or engineering the affected people cannot audit (not necessarily malicious). **Egregore**: a collective mind, like the "group chat" each information network forms; they nest. **Coverage**: how much of the memetic landscape someone can see and take part in. **Normie**: updates only after their community does, and acts as a stabilising filter. **Open/closed research gap**: studied openly vs happening privately. **Self-checking statement**: a claim whose correction either updates a good-faith speaker or exposes a bad-faith one. — [OMI glossary](https://defenderofbasic.github.io/open-memetics-institute/glossary.html)
- [SELF-REPORT] News page lists no unconference. It cites the University of Zurich Reddit persuasion experiment (April 2025) as the moment the open/closed gap went mainstream, and claims Brandon Duderstadt's January 2026 Johns Hopkins course as the first "memetics class". — [OMI news](https://defenderofbasic.github.io/open-memetics-institute/news.html)
- [SECONDARY] That course appears to be titled "Introduction to Computational Semiotics"; only OMI calls it memetics. — [byemarianne](https://byemarianne.substack.com/intro-to-computational-semiotics); [Latent Space wiki](https://latent.space/wiki/brandon-duderstadt/)
- [ASSERTED] "The Human Memome Project" proposes "a civilization-wide effort to map our own beliefs". Key lines: "There does not exist a neutral culture scientist" and "surfacing information in culture is NOT separate from changing it". Proposals, none implemented: decentralised "ambassadors" per culture, forkable replications, a GitHub-style repository of what is true from each ideology's perspective. Built: a "Shape of America" quiz prototype, never officially launched. The author says "I'm not sure yet" how to start. Inspiration: Blaise Agüera's *Who Are We Now*, a multi-year Mechanical Turk survey. — [Defender, Human Memome Project](https://defenderofthebasic.substack.com/p/the-human-memome-project)
- [ASSERTED, anecdotal] "Anatomy of an Internet Argument" rests on two hand-picked screenshots, author-judged success, no counts or controls. In one case the critic blocked the participant. Its one practical rule: show you want to understand, and restate their position so they can correct it. — [Defender, Anatomy](https://defenderofthebasic.substack.com/p/anatomy-of-an-internet-argument)
- [SELF-REPORT] The PhD repo claims group behaviour "can be predicted with great precision" and that private entities already do this; it presents no results. Named advisors: Slime Mold Time Mold, Park Doing. It does not say the author is enrolled anywhere. — [PhD repo](https://github.com/DefenderOfBasic/PhD)

### Inferences
- OMI's core worry and Dialogos' core design meet at one point: both hold that there is no view from nowhere. Defender's "ambassadors" is an unimplemented version of Dialogos' fidelity signal, where the described side signs. Dialogos is ahead here.
- Take from OMI: (a) a `dark/open` disclosure label on influence operations, and the idea that the Dialogos map should itself pass that test; (b) the "restate so they can correct it" move, which is already a cross-cluster note; (c) "coverage" as a person-level metric, which is close to B9's breadth measure; (d) the Janitor & CEO test as a fidelity-under-relay test (see the transmission-chain point below).
- Do not take the "hit-rate of changed minds" as a score. Dialogos' success is meta-consensus, not conversion, and that metric would reward persuasion.
- "Reflexivity" and the meditation-study remark (a larger sample may erase the effect) are the real intellectual content here. This is likely what Turquoise Sound means by the questions being interesting: they are questions about observing a system you are inside.

### Gaps
- No OMI legal status, budget or membership process confirmed. A SparkWell page quotes a Francisco Carvalho as "Project Lead at Open Memetics Institute" receiving 501(c)(3) funding ([SparkWell](https://www.antientropy.org/sparkwell), search snippet only). This person is not on the OMI members page read, and it may be a different body.
- Could not confirm the 2026 unconference happened, nor any "Open Memetics Journal". youregq.com returned a title only ("Your EDQ - Egregoric Diversity Quotient"), with no scoring or validation.
- OMI's tools page returned 404 at the guessed path; ORI's "launching" post 404'd. ORI's A/B/U system is therefore undescribed here. The [ORI site](https://open-research-institute.github.io) appeared in search, tagged "a space where prediction trumps paradigm", but was not read.

## Prosocial Engineering, Community Archive, and the X account

### Takeaway
Prosocial Engineering openly proposes open-source influence and shows no results on its home page. Its most valuable linked asset is the Community Archive: an opt-in, downloadable dataset of about 26 million tweets. That is the most concrete thing in this whole scene for Dialogos, with a heavy sampling skew. The X pages could not be read.

### Cited Findings
- [SELF-REPORT] prosocialengineering.org: Omar Shehata "owns and operates" this "open source marketing lab". Ronen V did the original memetics research; OhhShiny and Vitalik are named as funders. Thesis: large-scale social engineering cannot be regulated unless visible; each network should monitor itself and coordinate open-source social engineering that crowds out bad actors. Projects listed: Twitter Community Archive, Anatomy of an Internet Argument (described as an open source "psyop"), Good & Evil Words. No results, metrics or evaluations on the page. — [Prosocial Engineering](https://prosocialengineering.org/)
- [SELF-REPORT] GitHub DefenderOfBasic: display name "Defender", bio "science communication for scientists - prosocial engineering - ORI", X handle listed as **@DefenderOfBasic**, member of the Prosocial-Engineering org, pinned repos open-memetics-institute and good-and-evil-concepts. No real name stated. — [GitHub profile](https://github.com/DefenderOfBasic)
- [SELF-REPORT] Community Archive: 26,179,220 public tweets from 2,373 members at fetch time; members upload their own X data; daily Parquet export on GitHub releases; backed by the Survival and Flourishing Fund and Vitalik Buterin; apps include Birdseye, Strands, Conversation Map, Pairwise; research includes "a theory of tpot" by Epistemic Garden. The page does not state who runs it or the licence. — [community-archive.org](https://www.community-archive.org/)
- [SELF-REPORT] Stated goal is a public-domain dataset; API is read-only; intended uses include digital anthropology and "what are the origins of this idea / project / movement?". — [repo](https://github.com/TheExGenesis/community-archive) (via search snippet)
- [SELF-REPORT] Most contributors belong to the "tpot" community, and adoption of that term can be traced over time. — [Community Archive Substack](https://communityarchive.substack.com/p/exploring-historical-trends-in-the) (via search snippet)
- [SELF-REPORT] map-tpot computes PageRank, betweenness and community detection on follow relationships from the archive; no validation described. — [map-tpot](https://github.com/anantham/map-tpot)
- [SELF-REPORT] Good & Evil demo: cosine similarity of `nomic-embed-text-v1.5` vectors to "good" and "evil" pole words, in the browser. Its author says the aggregate cannot tell broad agreement from sharp disagreement between groups, and that results should be checked against surveys. Decade-tracking and campaign-effect testing are hypothetical. — [good-and-evil-concepts](https://github.com/DefenderOfBasic/good-and-evil-concepts)

### Inferences
- Community Archive fits Dialogos' consent model better than scraping, because contributors opted in. But it contains replies to and mentions of people who did not opt in, so the private-individual red line still applies. It also runs against D10/D14 (X and follows measure attention, not belief). Its best use is a **provenance and term-adoption test bench** for a single well-documented scene, not terrain building. Any use needs Benjamin's review of the data policy.
- Vitalik Buterin funds both Prosocial Engineering and the Community Archive (per those pages) and is a Slice 1 landmark in PE-01. Worth noting as a potential conflict if Dialogos uses this data for that encounter.
- The pole-projection trick addresses Benjamin's worry that embeddings cluster by topic. Poles define a stance-like axis. Its own caveat (no view of disagreement) points to the fix: fit or project per cluster and compare. See the Kozlowski finding below.

## hyperstitions.com: what it is

### Takeaway
It is a crypto token and market product, not a research group. It is the literal commercial form of "self-fulfilling prophecy as mechanism". Useful as a warning and a clean case, with no team, audit or methodology published. Do not assume any link to Jake Hartnell's "hyperstition markets" in B7.

### Cited Findings
- [SELF-REPORT] Describes itself as a coordination market on an EVM L1. The NO side is subsidised so YES is cheap; participants buy the token, bet YES and coordinate to make the outcome happen. Prediction markets "observe"; it aims to "manifest". Total volume $9.06M, 7 active markets, 3 resolved YES. Examples are token price targets, a launchpad market cap, and social markets on named accounts and mindshare rankings. NO had the largest volume in several, including a $7.42M launchpad market. No team, audit, paper or resolution method given; contact is X @hyperstiti0ns. — [hyperstitions.com](https://www.hyperstitions.com/)
- [SECONDARY] HST token trades on Monad; aggregator figures conflict (e.g. about $150K market cap and 512 holders on one Oct 2026 snapshot, $684K elsewhere). — [Alchemy listing](https://www.alchemy.com/dapps/hyperstitions); [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/price/hyperstitions/) (via search snippets; low reliability)

### Inferences
- The targets are almost all self-referential financial or attention goals. Their cheapest manipulation is buying the token. That fails B7's pro-social gameability test, and shows concretely why MOVE must never pay for a destination.
- $9M in volume is not evidence of coordination working. Three YES resolutions with no denominator or counterfactual say nothing about causation.

### Gaps
- No launch date, team, audit or third-party analysis found. No evidence for or against any relation to Hartnell's ENOVA or Incentives Markets.

## Meaning Alignment Institute (MAI)

### Takeaway
MAI is the most credentialed group here, and has one real empirical result: Moral Graph Elicitation. Its "thick models of value" is a position paper, not a finding. "Meaning hyperspace" and "semantic" do not appear in anything read, and "meaning preservation" is about values degrading up institutional stacks, not about embeddings.

### Cited Findings
- [SELF-REPORT] Non-profit. Joe Edelman is Founder & Director; core team Joe Edelman, Oliver Klingefjord, Ryan Lowe. Claims 100+ experts and support from ARIA, OpenAI, SFF, EIP, Longview. Outputs listed: Pax Machina, AGI Institutions, Full Stack Alignment, Market Intermediaries, What are Human Values?, Model Integrity, Democratic Fine-tuning. — [meaningalignment.org](https://www.meaningalignment.org)
- [PREPRINT] Klingefjord, Lowe, Edelman (2024): splits alignment into eliciting, reconciling and training. Moral Graph Elicitation has an LLM interview participants about values in context. Trial: representative sample of 500 Americans on three deliberately divisive prompts; 89.1% felt well represented; about 89% thought the final moral graph fair even when their own value was not voted wisest; "expert" values, such as those of women who had sought abortion advice, surfaced without defining experts in advance. — [arXiv 2404.10636](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.10636)
- [ASSERTED, position paper] Edelman et al. (Dec 2025, 30+ authors): thick models of value are a third option beside preference models and values-as-text, "a grammar for values" that constrains what can count as a value without dictating which. Values are "constitutive attentional policies". Norms need social acceptance, a cooperative role and legitimate origin. No new experiments; its only cited result is the MGE fairness finding. — [arXiv 2512.03399](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.03399v1)
- [ASSERTED] Meaning degradation: a desire for connection becomes engagement metrics, then DAU, then revenue; "what is meaningful is replaced by what is measurable". Thick models aim to keep value information intact at each level. — [Introducing FSA](https://meaningalignment.substack.com/p/introducing-full-stack-alignment); [paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.03399v1)
- [SELF-REPORT] Edelman's site lists "meaning-preserving markets" and Values, Preferences, Meaningful Choice (2022); no semantic or hyperspace language. — [nxhx.org](https://nxhx.org/)
- [SELF-REPORT] Pax Machina launched Aug 2026 from a Paris workshop on AGI institutions; the page says it is not an MAI project. — [launch post](https://meaningalignment.substack.com/p/launching-a-new-publication-pax-machina)

### Inferences
- Best import: the **fairness-despite-losing** measure. "Did you find this fair although your value did not win?" is a direct, cheap meta-consensus survey item for MEET.
- MGE (LLM interview, then a graph with humans voting on it) is a working template for gathering Sacred Values slots from opted-in humans, without inferring them. It is complementary to landmark-based extraction.
- Stack degradation is a warning aimed at MOVE: a single public score is exactly the compression MAI warns about. It also parallels Benjamin's single-metric critique (A4 P5).
- "Attentional policy" gives a definition of a value that is observable in what a person treats as salient. That is worth testing against the Limberg Sacred Values slot.
- Do not conflate MAI "thick" with Geertz "thick". Nothing read links them. MAI's abstract grounding is Taylor and Chang. The Geertz sense is thick with participants' own meanings; the MAI sense is thick with normative structure. Dialogos may want both.
- "Meaning hyperspace" may be Turquoise Sound's own phrase, or nearer to computational semiotics and embedding work (Duderstadt, Good & Evil) than to MAI.

### Gaps
- "Meaning hyperspace" not found anywhere: not on MAI pages, nxhx.org, or a phrase search. Only the first 100,000 of 179,697 characters of the FSA paper were read, so absence in its tail is unconfirmed. Ask Turquoise Sound for the source.
- Jim Rutt EP 325 transcript returned HTTP 503. full-stack-alignment.ai returned a title only. MGE follow-up or replication not found; the trial is one US sample and self-reported by its authors, in a preprint. Reconciliation mechanics not read.

## Tom Murray

### Takeaway
Murray is the strongest bridge for Dialogos' stated problem: how to measure integration as a *capacity* rather than a stage. They combine integral and developmental theory with an NSF-funded programme that operationalised "social deliberative skills", with a coding scheme, text analysis, and a reported large experimental effect from simple reflective tools. The work dates from 2010–2016, and precise numbers were not retrieved.

### Cited Findings
- [SELF-REPORT] Senior Research Fellow, UMass Amherst CS (2011–present); Meridian University faculty; Associate Editor, *Integral Review*; PI on NSF "SoCS: The Fourth Party" ($750,000, 2010–2014); PI on MetaIntegral grants "Automated Text Analysis for Assessing Group Developmental Levels" ($10,000 each, 2014 and 2016). Publications include "On the development of beliefs vs. capacities" (2015), "Contemplative Dialogue Practices" (2015), "Integralist mental models of adult development: Provisos from a Users Guide" (2011). — [Murray CV](https://www.tommurray.prodox.org/cv.html)
- [SHOWN, conference] Social deliberative skills: the capacity to deal productively with heterogeneous goals, values or perspectives. Components in the 2013 framework: perspective taking, perspective seeking, perspective monitoring (including meta-dialogue), perspective weighing. A Social Deliberative Coding Scheme was used by annotators, and computational predictors correlated significantly with annotated skill. — [UMass TR 2012-027](https://web.cs.umass.edu/publication/docs/2012/UM-CS-2012-027.pdf); [AIED 2013](https://ceur-ws.org/Vol-1009/1001.pdf) (via search snippets)
- [SHOWN, as the authors report it] College students using Mediem's reflective tools showed a significant, large effect on social deliberative skill support vs a control group. The three tools are simple: Story (what is at stake for you), Conversation Thermometer (rate the quality of the conversation), and Contribution Tags. — [Murray et al. 2013 dashboard paper](https://web.cs.umass.edu/publication/docs/2013/UM-CS-2013-014.pdf); [Springer chapter](https://rd.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-3-642-39371-6_36.pdf)
- [SHOWN, formative only] The Facilitators Dashboard uses about 20 LIWC categories, including `certain`, `tentative`, `assent`, `negate`, first/second person, plus expert-added lists: always-never, should, and question words. Dialogue speech acts that may mark turning points: changes of mind, peer-initiated mediation, apologies, high emotion, acknowledgements. Evaluation is qualitative. Three mediators over 18 months and ten contracted experts found it "extremely useful", but the interface was "too busy". One facilitator noticed a predominantly white class discussing Zimmerman far more than Martin, from a word cloud. — [same paper](https://web.cs.umass.edu/publication/docs/2013/UM-CS-2013-014.pdf)
- [ASSERTED, theory] Epistemic drives are cognitive tendencies influencing how knowledge is produced or shared, such as desire for certainty; negative capability is the counterweight. Murray (2006) says integral theory points to leading ways of handling indeterminacy, while integral practitioners' knowledge-building often shows the same limits as traditional discourse. — [Murray 2011](https://doaj.org/article/b0389106274141f7a1486d409b187c19); [Murray 2006](https://integral-review.org/documents/Murray,%20Collaborative%20Knowledge%202,%202006.pdf); [2010 slides](https://pt.slideshare.net/ITC2013/2013-itc-slides-murray-distrib) (via search snippets)

### Inferences
- This directly answers A4 tension 4 and risk "bias in the gradient": measure **capacity, not stage**. SD skills are behaviours in text, with no ladder of persons. Murray's own "beliefs vs. capacities" title suggests the same split, though the paper itself was not read.
- Cheap candidate SEE dimension: **epistemic posture** per landmark, from `tentative` vs `certain`, always-never words, and question rate in their own transcripts. It needs validation, and LIWC is shallow by the authors' own account.
- Candidate MOVE or MEET signals: perspective-seeking questions, meta-dialogue, apologies, changes of mind. Pair with a warning: once rewarded, these are easy to perform.
- The three Mediem features are nearly free to build into MEET, and they are the only intervention in this set with a claimed controlled effect.
- Murray is a plausible reviewer of a capacities rubric. No outreach without Benjamin's say-so.

### Gaps
- socialdeliberativeskills.com did not resolve. N, inter-rater reliability and effect-size numbers were not retrieved; the "large effect" is the authors' phrase. "Contemplative Dialogue Practices" (2015) and "beliefs vs. capacities" were not read in full, so their method is undescribed. No post-2016 work on LLMs was found.

## Society Library: method, the "Platonic" critique, and the cultural-theory difference

### Takeaway
The Society Library maps what is *said* and how claims relate logically. Cultural theory asks who gets to say it, what goes without saying, and how a claim is read by different groups. That is a real gap, and Dialogos partly shares it, because its neutral proposition is the same kind of object. The fair framing is complement, not rival. The Library itself has started addressing this since November 2025.

### Cited Findings
- [SELF-REPORT] Nonpartisan 501(c)(3) in Orlando; lists AI fact-checking, a claim collector, autonomous research agents, debate maps, teaching at 32 universities; has worked with or been funded by Foresight, Helena, OpenAI, Meta, Poynter's IFCN; $10k bounties Feb 2026. — [societylibrary.org](https://www.societylibrary.org/)
- [SELF-REPORT] Debate mapping is branded "The Great American Debate" as a nod to its US focus. It first discovers the fundamental questions society is debating ("descriptive emergent structuring"), then fleshes out answers, then links arguments through 20+ logical relationships. Topics: Nuclear (170+ sub-topics), Climate (270+), Elections (80+), COVID-19 (500+), AI. — [Debate Maps](https://www.societylibrary.org/debate-mapping-program)
- [SELF-REPORT] Sources include memes, satire, film, chat threads; scope is English-speaking, US-centric issues. The stated purpose is a database of "society's ideas, ideologies, and worldviews". — [Methodologies](https://www.societylibrary.org/methodologies)
- [SELF-REPORT] Joyce (May 2026): 5,862 items from over 5,000 references in about 8,000 hours for Diablo Canyon, unfinished. Relations include "presupposes" and "makes less probable". Precedents cited are Aristotle's dialectic and Socratic elenchus. Joyce concedes frames often matter more than facts but does not build that in; culture, ideology and power are not addressed. Goal includes "computable policy". — [Joyce](https://justjamiejoyce.substack.com/p/your-attempt-to-refute-argument-maps)
- [SELF-REPORT] Tools: Rose (top-down prompt cascade) and Juniper (bottom-up embedding clusters into "idea bundles" for analyst review). "Deliberative ontological structure" is the phrase used. — [Updates](https://www.societylibrary.org/society-library-updates)
- [SELF-REPORT] Nov 2025, "Weaving In More Epistemological Paradigms in 2026": a co-designed epistemology taxonomy and map, workshops with knowledge holders, a formal process for soliciting perspectives directly, after Yale deliberations involving ancestral knowledge systems. It rejects cultural relativism and says its work "does not serve to advance any ideology, political framing, or body politic". — [Updates](https://www.societylibrary.org/society-library-updates)
- [SELF-REPORT] Joyce's framing of the suite as a "legislative branch in a box". — [LinkedIn snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/justjamiejoyce/)
- [SECONDARY] Hall: decoding does not follow from encoding; positions are dominant-hegemonic, negotiated, oppositional. Critics: polysemy, and assuming all media encode dominant ideology. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encoding/decoding_model_of_communication)
- [SECONDARY] Laclau: separate demands form a chain of equivalence through a shared opposition, and one signifier comes to stand for all of them (Solidarność). Empty concerns building identity against a stable frontier; floating concerns a shifting frontier. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empty_signifier)
- [SECONDARY] Gramsci: hegemony works by consent, making a worldview seem natural; "common sense" hides how one's position is produced; "war of position" is the long cultural contest. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_hegemony)
- [SECONDARY] Bourdieu: fields are arenas of struggle over species of capital; capital only exists relative to a field; habitus is learned disposition; illusio is acknowledgement that the stakes are worth playing for. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_theory_(sociology))
- [SECONDARY] Geertz: culture as a web of meaning; thick description records context as participants understand it; speak to subjects rather than for them. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thick_description)
- [SHOWN] Kozlowski, Taddy, Evans (ASR 2019): vector differences correspond to cultural dimensions; projections validated against surveys and labelled historical data; they argue embeddings operationalise and extend Bourdieu's field theory, and that correspondence analysis is a low-dimensional special case. — [ASR](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/0003122419877135); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09288)
- [SHOWN] Boutyline & Vaisey (AJS 2017): belief systems as correlation networks with measurable centrality; ideological identity reported as central. — [record](https://katalog.lib.cas.cz/KNAV/EdsRecord/edsair,edsair.doi...........b716abbebfbb0a606340e5e949ceb503); [Network Science CFP citing them with Goldberg 2011](https://resolve-he.cambridge.org/core/journals/network-science/announcements/call-for-papers/special-issue-call-for-papers-network-approaches-to-attitudes-and-beliefs)
- [PREPRINT] Boutyline's correlational class analysis modifies relational class analysis, which finds shared schemas in survey data. — [CV](https://www.ocf.berkeley.edu/~andrei/cv.html)
- Caution on embeddings: a paper warns scientists to define what kind of meaning embeddings capture. — [arXiv 2107.10413](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.10413)

### Inferences
"Platonic" reading, as this researcher reconstructs it (Turquoise Sound's actual wording is not available):
- The Library's object is the claim-in-itself: one ID, many phrasings, one logical place. That treats the proposition as an ideal form behind its instances. "Platonic" is loose, since Joyce's own lineage is Aristotelian and Socratic. "Logicist" or "propositionalist" would be more exact.
- **Dialogos has the same assumption.** The "neutral proposition" pipeline stage is exactly this move. If the critique lands, it lands on Dialogos' step 5 too. What Dialogos brings is not a different extraction, but clusters, ratification by the described side, and the option to add the layers below.
- Note also that Joyce's "presupposes" relation is a hook: it is already where doxa could live.

## Is there computational or empirical memetics now? Which traditions give testable methods?

### Takeaway
Strict gene-like memetics failed as a science. What survives works under other names: computational diffusion tracking, cultural attraction, transmission-chain experiments, and phylogenetics. For Dialogos, the testable tools are variant tracking, transmission chains, and attractor analysis. "Meme" is a fine word for the public, and a weak unit for the schema.

### Cited Findings
- [SECONDARY] Dawkins' memetics has popular attention but little scientific standing. Critics: imitation is too error-prone, ideas lack traceable lineages, cultural traits are not particulate; Sober says selective models add little. — [SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/evolution-cultural/)
- [SECONDARY] *Journal of Memetics* ran 1997–2005 and ceased, the relaunch never occurring. Gould: "meaningless metaphor". Blackmore (1999) on imitation and "meme machines". Internet memetics is more distributed empirical studies than a theory; Shifman (2014) defines memes by content, form and stance. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memetics)
- [SECONDARY] Edmonds (2005) argued narrow memetics failed, with work continuing under broad approaches and other names. His supporting plot is disputed. The Edmonds text itself was not read. — [New Savanna](https://new-savanna.blogspot.com/2015/03/memetics-is-dead-but-whats-study-of.html?m=1); [Masaryk study](https://is.muni.cz/publication/1673735/WhyDidMemeticsFailComparativeCaseStudy.pdf)
- [SECONDARY] Dennett defends loose memes as "ways" of doing things; Boyd & Richerson model biases without needing replicators (conformist, prestige); Sperber's attractors come from reconstructive learning, possibly local to communities; Henrich's cultural learning and Mesoudi's Darwinian synthesis (criticised by Ingold). Testable examples: Henrich & Broesch on prestige bias in Fiji; Gray & Watts' phylogenies on moralising gods. — [SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/evolution-cultural/)
- [SHOWN] Claidière, Scott-Phillips & Sperber (2014): cultural attraction is populational and only sometimes selectional; variation is intentional and less random than in biology. — [Durham](https://durham-repository.worktribe.com/output/1466956/how-darwinian-is-cultural-evolution)
- [SHOWN] Leskovec, Backstrom, Kleinberg (KDD 2009): quoted phrases tracked through about 90M articles; mutated variants clustered; about a 2.5-hour news-to-blog lag. — [Berkman summary](https://archive.blogs.harvard.edu/mediaberkman/2010/02/16/jure-leskovec-on-meme-tracking-and-the-dynamics-of-the-news-cycle/); [review](https://liner.com/review/memetracking-and-dynamics-news-cycle)
- [SHOWN] Adamic, Lento, Adar, Ng: thousands of Facebook memes replicated hundreds of millions of times; variants follow a Yule process; edit distance grows along cascades (iterative imperfect copying); **subgroups pass along the variant that matches their beliefs or culture**. — [arXiv 1402.6792](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.6792)
- [SHOWN] Transmission chains identify content biases; Acerbi et al. 2022: negative content favoured in chains and in sharing; threat only in chains; disgust in neither. Extending chain results to real-life dynamics is not straightforward. — [Mesoudi & Whiten](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2607337); [Human Nature 2022](https://link.springer.com/doi/10.1007/s12110-022-09423-1); [PMC9250454](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9250454)

### Inferences

| Tradition | Status | Use for Dialogos |
|---|---|---|
| Dawkins, Blackmore strict replicator | Metaphor | Vocabulary only |
| Dennett loose memes | Philosophical | Framing |
| Distin | Not verified | None yet |

## Hyperstition: lineage, modern operational use, and detection from data

### Takeaway
A hyperstition is detectable only as a causal claim, not as a pattern: an idea about the future must change behaviour in ways that fundamentals do not explain, and thereby produce that future. The finance literature gives working designs. Nothing in the memetics scene does. Dialogos can do a modest candidate-flagging version, and should avoid labelling anything confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- [Primary interview] Land: "a positive feedback circuit including culture as a component", "the experimental (techno-)science of self-fulfilling prophecies". Superstitions are "merely false beliefs"; hyperstitions function causally to bring about their own reality. Example: the fictional idea of Cyberspace drew investment and became real. The piece contains no detection method. — [Carstens interview](https://www.orphandriftarchive.com/articles/hyperstition-an-introduction/)
- [Brief B7] Hartnell's hyperstition markets, later "Incentives Markets"; Merton and Biggs; performative prediction; pro-social gameability. — [B7](/Users/benjaminlife/dev/schismogenesis/docs/research/B7-girard-hyperstition-state-machine.md)
- [PREPRINT, published versions unverified] Cookson et al.: banks with high pre-run Twitter exposure lost more in the SVB run (about 4.3 points in the earliest version, 6.6 points for the top tercile in later ones). Hourly attention predicts losses, especially for high-run-risk banks. Effects are strongest for contagion keywords and likely depositors. Negative *sentiment* did not amplify run risk in the early version. — [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/abstract=4422754); [FDIC-hosted version](https://www.fdic.gov/system/files/2024-07/cookson-paper.pdf)
- [PREPRINT] Semenova & Winkler: WallStreetBets views partly reflect peers; discussion rises and falls in an epidemic-like pattern; IV identification in an earlier version; later version estimates price effects directly. — [arXiv 2104.01847](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.01847v5)
- [PREPRINT] Goetzmann, Kim, Shiller (NBER 2022): crash narratives spread once in news and predict volatility; survey data separate narrativity from response to fundamentals; "pure narrativity" measure. — [NBER 30195](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:nbr:nberwo:30195)
- [Conceptual] Shiller's *Narrative Economics*: stories spread like epidemics. — [Princeton UP](https://press.princeton.edu/node/42803)
- [Journalism, sources conflict] Truth Terminal and GOAT: Infinite Backrooms (March 2024), X account (June 2024), GOAT launched by an anonymous creator (Oct 2024), peak cap reported $290–450M; posts approved by Ayrey; base model reported differently. Ayrey called it "a study in memetic contagion". Ayrey is on OMI's periphery list. — [Blockworks timeline](https://blockworks.co/news/0xresearch-newsletter-goatse-memecoin); [Know Your Meme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/sites/truth-terminal); [OMI members](https://defenderofbasic.github.io/open-memetics-institute/members.html)

### Inferences
Proposed test. To call X a hyperstition candidate, require all of:
1. **Future-state claim** with a resolvable target, made at time t.
2. **Precedence**: the claim's spread leads behaviour, not just outcome.
3. **Behavioural mediation**: named actions by holders, such as money, migration, hiring, building.
4. **Excess over fundamentals**: a reference-class or control comparison. The hard one: an accurate forecast also precedes its outcome.
5. **Reflexivity marker**: holders cite the belief itself as a reason to act ("others believe it, so...").
6. **Exogenous variation** where possible: differential exposure (Cookson's design), or instruments (Semenova).

Without 4 or 6, label it "candidate". Without 5, it may be plain forecasting.

- On Dialogos data, Slice 1 could add a `future_claim` tag and a `commitment_vs_forecast` tag on propositions. That is cheap: transcripts already contain "AGI by 2027"-type claims. Tracking outcomes comes later.
- PE-01 (e/acc × d/acc) is a good pilot, since both are explicit about shaping the future through belief. Treat this as a hypothesis, and label it publicly with care, since calling a person's stated view a hyperstition is itself a contestable placement.
- Cookson's attention-over-sentiment result gently supports D10's demotion of follows to attention, but also says attention is causal in crises. Keep the attention overlay.
- Case strength: SVB, WSB, narrative economics are the only ones with identification. Truth Terminal and hyperstitions.com are stories and products. The Atlas's narrative framing should not be mistaken for evidence.
- Benign reflexivity (B7) stands: an audit of Dialogos' own effect is the same test pointed inward (exposed vs control).
