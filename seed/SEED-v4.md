Author of record: Benjamin Life (@omniharmonic). SEED-v3 plus the g7 (online subcultures) and g8 (holistic sweep) gap fills and a small QA gap fill, 2026-10-31. Machine-readable data: `SEED-v4.json`. Decisions and audit: `QA-REPORT-v4.md`. Open checks: `VERIFICATION-QUEUE-v4.json`. Built by `scripts/build_v4.py` from `scripts/decisions_v4.py` over `SEED-v3.json`.

## Summary

- **234 niches, 817 landmarks** (SEED-v3: 220 / 771). New in v4: 14 niches, 46 landmark records. 4 alternates held out; 1 excluded (unchanged).
- Verification: **592 verified-active (72%)**, 222 pending, 3 stale.
- Women 30% of landmarks (v3: 29%). Bridgers 27% by primary role (v3: 26%).
- 53 mirror pairs (7 new).
- Flagged for Benjamin: 20 landmarks (5 conflict-of-interest) and 7 niches (outside reviewer).
- g8 coverage matrix: 154 outside-taxonomy categories checked; 150 covered (11 former gaps closed in v4), 2 still gaps.
