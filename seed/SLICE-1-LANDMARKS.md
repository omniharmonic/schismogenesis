### AI safety / existential-risk community  `ai-safety-xrisk`

### Populist AI backlash (anti-transhumanist right & AI-accountability coalition)  `populist-ai-backlash`

A cross-partisan political front, loudest on the populist right, that opposes the AI industry's agenda: federal preemption of state AI laws, data-center buildout paid for by ratepayers, AI companions for children and job displacement. Its right wing frames transhumanism as a spiritual threat to human nature; its policy wing works for liability rules, child-safety laws and super PACs that back regulation.

### Progress studies / abundance  `progress-studies-abundance`

An intellectual movement that asks why progress happens and why it has slowed, and pushes to remove the barriers that hold it back: permitting, NIMBYism, slow science funding. In 2025 'Abundance' (Klein and Thompson) carried it into center-left politics, while progress-studies institutes kept a nonpartisan, history-of-technology tone.
