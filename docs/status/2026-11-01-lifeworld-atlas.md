# Lifeworld Atlas: first region

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-01-lifeworld-atlas-design.md`. Method: ADR-035.

| Step | Result |
|---|---|
| Corpus | 42 new works from 14 people; 93 works and 27 people in all |
| Claims | 3,947 extracted overall; 2,158 passed the gates |
| Ideas | 360 after merging, across five layers |
| Holdings | 1,292 agreed by two readers; 336 dropped as stretched; none reversed; 80 added from 734 proposed |
| Inferred roots | 49 of 91 links judged required |
| Links | 494 typed links after a blind check of all 577 |
| Coverage | About 10% of person and idea pairs |

## Findings
- AI safety and AI critics agree on every shared family about knowing and the world, and split on
  what language models are.
- Accelerationism and AI safety share a picture of AI, oppose on 4 of 5 action families, and also
  split at the roots. The hypothesis that they fork only at values was half right.
- E/acc is not a school here. Verdon and Andreessen sit with builders, and Land with power realists.
- Hawley groups with Bender, Gebru and Hao across party lines.

## Limits
- Provisional machine reading. No person has seen or ratified it.
- About three works per person. Dates mean first appearance in this sample.
- Fine-grained comparison is thin, and nine schools is the top of the tested range.
- Sense gaps mix change of subject with change of meaning.
- Reasons against belonging is a lead: missing links may be reader coverage.
