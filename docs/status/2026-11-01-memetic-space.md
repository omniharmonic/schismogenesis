## Findings
- Same-or-different scoring made Yarvin a false general outlier. Graded distance puts him loosely
  with Thiel and Balaji, while still far from Vance (0.67, together in 2% of reruns).
- Most rivalry is tension, not opposition: 22 of 109 position pairs are truly opposed.
- Stance reverses what counts suggest. Yudkowsky attacks "AI safety"; Land attacks orthogonality yet
  embraces instrumental convergence; Buterin attacks "techno-capital", which Andreessen builds on.

## Open problems
- Seven questions mix different kinds of answer, such as likelihood and source of danger. Their
  distances compare unlike things. They should be split.
- The role gate saw 500 characters either side. Thiel and Land need whole-essay context.
- The fidelity audit has not been rerun since the role gate.
- Meme stance rests on up to 3 passages. Sampling should grow where stance is low confidence.
- Time is shown per sampled work, not as drift within a person. Thiel's arc is the obvious test.
- Thirteen hand-picked people cannot support real clusters. Cross-niche people are the next need.
