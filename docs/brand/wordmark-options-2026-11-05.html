The quietest option and the cheapest: no new typeface, just the lab's own Plex Condensed set lowercase with a solid text cursor after it. The cursor is the internet in one shape, and it says the thing the lab asks of people: your turn to write. It reads as a console prompt, which fits the ops-console furniture already on the site. The risk is that a blinking or block cursor is a familiar tech-startup move, so it's the least ownable of the three.

The closest to Palantir: capitals, wide tracking, and a small symbol that does the brand's work. The symbol is the selection frame the map already draws around a face you pick, with one node inside it: a person, singled out to be understood rather than piled on. It also reads as a screenshot crop, the way memes get captured and passed around. It is the most institutional and the easiest to scale to new debates, because the symbol can sit beside any sub-brand. It is also the coolest in temperature, and its internet-culture hint is the faintest.

The first e is turned to face its twin across the m: "mɘme". That is the whole theory in a glyph. Girard's rivals become mirror images of each other, Bateson's split is a mirror answering a mirror, and the lab's answer is to face the other without becoming their copy. The expanded, tightly set sans gives it the precision and weight of an institution. The reversed letter is the internet in it: the same letter play as a backwards meme caption, done once and quietly. The icon is the pair alone, two letters facing each other, which works for any future debate, not just AI.

My pick: C, Doubles.

It's the only one of the three that couldn't belong to another company, because its single idea is this lab's idea. It stays readable as "antimemes" at nav size, and its icon holds up at 16px. It carries no AI imagery, so it can stretch from AI policy to the wider pop-culture landscape. Cost: it brings in a fourth typeface (Archivo, for the wordmark only), which the brand file currently rules out, so choosing it means amending docs/brand/BRAND.md. If you'd rather keep to the existing faces, B is the runner-up and can be rebuilt in Plex Condensed with little loss.
